# WhereNext

WhereNext helps people discover destinations and organise places they are considering visiting.

## Language

**Destination**:
A place someone could choose as the base for a holiday, distinct from the airport used to reach it.
_Avoid_: Airport, itinerary

**Destination catalogue**:
The collection of destinations known to WhereNext and available for discovery or recommendation.
_Avoid_: Search results, shortlist

**Trip estimate**:
An indicative cost for the travelling group's holiday, with retrieved prices and estimated spending shown separately. It is not a guaranteed package price.
_Avoid_: Quote, booking total

**Preference fit**:
How well evidence about a destination supports a traveller's stated interests and trip preferences. Unknown information does not establish a fit or a mismatch.
_Avoid_: Affordability, availability

**Daily spending allowance**:
The traveller's planned spending per person per day on food, local transport and activities, separate from flights and accommodation.
_Avoid_: Live local prices

**Shortlist**:
A private, named collection of destinations owned by one person. It may group alternatives for one holiday or places sharing a theme, and a destination may belong to several shortlists.
_Avoid_: Trip, itinerary
