# External travel data sources

Research checked on 18 October 2026. This report explains the source investigation and its limits; [the specification](spec.md) owns product decisions. Provider prices, access terms and API contracts can change and must be checked before implementation.

**Production recommendation withdrawn, 20 October 2026.** The user requires free-only ongoing access and rejects the SerpApi 250-search monthly allowance. The comparison below is historical research, not a direction to build on SerpApi. See the [production review](production-provider-review.md) and [evidence/accommodation review](non-flight-provider-review.md). Provider limits must be assessed per full planning session before implementation; request caps alone do not make a small free tier suitable.

## Discovery is different from route pricing

Destination discovery asks where someone could go from an origin. A route-pricing request asks for flights to a destination already chosen. Searching every possible destination separately multiplies request use and latency. A flight API also does not establish activities, atmosphere, climate suitability or the rest of the holiday cost.

SerpApi's published allowance was 250 free searches/month and 50/hour; the next plan was $25/month for 1,000. Successful uncached requests share the allowance across engines. One discovery plus accommodation searches for five cities could therefore consume six requests, or roughly 41 such sessions per 250 requests before other work. This is illustrative arithmetic, not measured application usage. Paid access is not approved. [Pricing and counting](https://serpapi.com/pricing)

## Accommodation and local spending

Google Hotels through SerpApi documents exact check-in/out, currency and adult counts, which default to two if omitted. It distinguishes nightly rates from stay totals and can return fee-related fields. Room allocation, bed/private-room suitability, complete fees and onward-link retention still need verification. A cheapest property's amount does not establish a representative city-wide holiday cost. The Explore field named hotel price has an insufficiently defined basis for arithmetic and was absent in the inspected discovery samples. [Hotels contract](https://serpapi.com/google-hotels-api), [Explore contract](https://serpapi.com/google-travel-explore-api)

No suitable free automatic source for daily food, transport and activity spending was verified. Numbeo's commercial reuse needs licensing and living-cost indices still need a travel-spending model. The specification instead uses an editable allowance; do not manufacture destination prices. [Numbeo terms](https://www.numbeo.com/common/terms_of_use.jsp), [index definitions](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/cpi_explained.jsp)

| GeoNames                 | Gazetteer identities, names, countries and coordinates; useful as an optional identity cross-check.                                                                                        | Does not supply complete travel suitability or costing fields. | Open-Meteo               | Historical coordinate-based temperature/precipitation could support weather-specific evidence.                                                                                             | Historical climate does not prove best travel months or a forecast. The free hosted API is non-commercial with limits and no uptime guarantee; commercial access differs. It was not tested in the observed feasibility calls. [Historical API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), [pricing](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing)                                                                                    |

Descriptions, imagery, structured records and observed prices have different reuse conditions. Successful API access alone does not settle storage/display rights, attribution or refresh policy. Adopt sources only after those conditions and data meanings have been reviewed; retain provenance and distinguish documented contracts from observed sample behaviour.
