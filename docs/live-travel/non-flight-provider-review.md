# Non-flight provider review

Follow-up: [authenticated response check](replacement-response-check.md) records the 20 October live API probes. Access now works for both candidates; production suitability remains subject to the limitations recorded there. The documentation-only findings below predate those probes.

Reviewed 20 October 2026. This is source research, not a successful production integration or an account approval. No new accounts, keys, paid calls or booking transactions were created for this review.

## Destination evidence

**Geoapify Places is a credible replacement candidate for SerpApi place searches.** Its free plan permits commercial production use, provides **3,000 credits per day** and permits up to **5 requests per second**, without a credit card. Places searches returning up to 20 places cost one credit; larger results cost one credit per block of 20. Place Details and other endpoints have separate costs. The quota is shared across the account's API usage, not a fresh allowance per feature. [Pricing](https://www.geoapify.com/pricing/), [credit costs](https://www.geoapify.com/pricing-details/), [Places documentation](https://apidocs.geoapify.com/docs/places/)

A cold search enriching five destinations with three category queries each, limited to 20 results, would consume 15 Places credits: **200 such sessions per day** before geocoding, retries or other API usage. That is an illustrative bound, not a measured capacity guarantee. Cache destination evidence independently of dated travel prices so identical places can reuse results across planning sessions.

The API supplies geographic/category evidence such as museums, restaurants, historic sights and parks. It does not supply current hotel prices, full destination guides, verified seasonal suitability or a definitive judgement that a destination is relaxing. Missing results mean unknown evidence, not evidence that an activity does not exist. Representative small towns and cities still need coverage checks. [Places capabilities and categories](https://www.geoapify.com/places-api/)

Geoapify explicitly permits caching, storing and redistributing Places results. Keep source, retrieval time and attribution with the evidence. OpenStreetMap attribution is required, plus Geoapify attribution on the free plan; observe underlying data licences when publishing derived datasets. Do not multiply accounts to evade limits. Use a local daily credit ceiling and bounded concurrency: the terms allow suspension or overage charges if limits are exceeded. [Places reuse FAQ](https://www.geoapify.com/places-api/), [terms](https://www.geoapify.com/terms-and-conditions/), [OpenStreetMap copyright and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

**Gate before selection:** obtain a free key, confirm current account limits, test representative categories/locations, validate attribution and storage design, and record measured credits. No claim of tested Geoapify data quality has been made here.

## Accommodation

**No unconditional free production hotel-search replacement was established.** Do not put a new hotel provider into implementation acceptance criteria as a settled choice.

| Nuitee Connect / LiteAPI | Rates supports dated, multi-room occupancy searches. Core Rates → Prebook → Book is free only subject to terms and a reasonable look-to-book ratio. ## Delivery consequence

Keep the whole-holiday product goal, but make live accommodation contingent on a passed provider-access and economics gate. If no affordable approved source is available, offer an explicitly traveller-supplied accommodation estimate with dated hotel-search links. Mark the total as an estimate and do not claim that accommodation is retrieved or available. This is a fallback proposal requiring product agreement, not a silently approved scope change.

Do not replace SerpApi's 250-search dependency with an equally unverified “free” booking API. Preserve normalised cost/evidence models and existing dated-planning work; adapters, budgets and failure handling should be specific to the provider actually selected.
